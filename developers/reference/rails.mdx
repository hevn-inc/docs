A payment method is the bank network a payin or payout uses, named by a value such as `sepa` or `ach`. Read what one client can use with `GET /dapi/v1/banks`, and what one contact can receive with `GET /dapi/v1/contacts/{contactId}/capabilities`.

[Virtual accounts](/developers/virtual-accounts) defines product, rail, virtual account and account details.

## Products

Request a virtual account with `POST /dapi/v1/banks` and one of these products. The payer sends the listed currency over one of the listed methods.

| `product` | Methods | Currency |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `us` | `ach`, `fedwire` | USD |
| `swift` | `swift` | USD |
| `eu` | `sepa` | EUR |
| `uk` | `ukfps` | GBP |
| `uae` | `uaefts` | AED |
| `br` | `pix` | BRL |
| `mx` | `spei` | MXN |
| `co` | `breb` | COP |
| `au` | `npp` | AUD |
| `ng` | `nip` | NGN |

`GET /dapi/v1/banks` returns the products one client can request, with `currency` and `method` for each. Use the response in code, not this table.

The `product` enum in the API reference has more codes than this table. A code the client cannot request is absent from its `GET /dapi/v1/banks` response, and `POST /dapi/v1/banks` with it returns `403 rail_not_available`.

Each `requisites` object and each contact has a `holder`. Its `type` is `individual` with `firstName` and `lastName`, or `business` with `businessName`. The holder does not replace the identifiers.

## Availability

| Narrowed by | Effect | Returned as |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Your integrator agreement ([Going live](/developers/sandbox#what-hevn-must-enable)) | Which products HEVN enables for you | `403 rail_not_available` on `POST /dapi/v1/banks` |
| The client's residency and legal form | Which of those products the client can request | The product is absent from `GET /dapi/v1/banks` |
| The account named in `accountId` | Which products can deliver to an external account or a sub account ([Virtual accounts](/developers/virtual-accounts#deliver-to-an-account)) | `409 account_destination_unsupported` on `POST /dapi/v1/banks`, `403 rail_not_available` on `GET /dapi/v1/banks/{product}` |
| The client's verification state | Whether a requested virtual account can become `active` | `blockers[]` on the record: `kyb_required`, `verification_required`, `terms_required` |
| Rail status | Whether the rail accepts payments now | `503 provider_unavailable`, or `channel_unavailable` in a contact's `blockers[]` |

## Chain codes

A chain code names the chain in `crypto.chain` of a wallet [contact](/developers/payouts#save-an-address-as-a-contact) and in `originChainId` of a quoted on-chain [payin](/developers/payins#quote-an-on-chain-transfer).

A transfer to `baseSmartWallet` and a quoted on-chain payin need no virtual account and no verification. The client must be `ready`.

Read [`GET /dapi/v1/chains`](/api-reference/catalog/list-supported-chains-and-tokens) at startup and do not hardcode the codes below.

Chain codes: `eth`, `bsc`, `avax`, `op`, `arb`, `pol`, `sol`, `base`, `gnosis`, `tron`, `xrp`, `zec`, `doge`, `ltc`, `btc`, `cardano`, `bera`, `sui`, `near`, `near_intents`, `xlayer`, `plasma`, `ton`, `stellar`, `monad`, `starknet`, `aptos`, `adi`, `bch`.

A payout to a chain other than Base is [routed](/developers/payouts#a-routed-payout-is-priced).

## Fees

A fee or rate in this API is either declared before a payment exists or quoted on one payment. Rates are set per integrator. Ask HEVN for yours.

### Declared and quoted

| | Declared | Quoted |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Meaning | A rate for a product or a contact | The terms of one payment |
| Returned by | `GET /dapi/v1/banks`, `GET /dapi/v1/banks/{product}/rate`, `GET /dapi/v1/contacts/{contactId}/capabilities`, `POST /dapi/v1/payouts/preview`, `GET /dapi/v1/rates` | `quote` on `POST /dapi/v1/payins` and `POST /dapi/v1/payouts` |
| Use | Display before the customer commits | Charge and reconcile |
| Binding | No. It can change | Yes, until `expiresAt` |

Do not bill from a stored declared rate.

### Fee fields

| Field | Returned by | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `feeBps`, `minimumDeposit` | `GET /dapi/v1/banks` | The payin fee is taken from the converted amount. A payment below `minimumDeposit` is returned to the payer. |
| `feeBps`, `fixedFee`, `minAmount`, `maxAmount` | `options[]` on `GET /dapi/v1/contacts/{contactId}/capabilities` | Indicative price and amount range for one contact. See [Payouts](/developers/payouts#save-a-contact). |
| `feeAmount`, `feeCurrency`, `rate` | `quote` on a payin or a payout | The binding terms. See [Payins](/developers/payins) and [Payouts](/developers/payouts). |
| `expiresAt` | `quote` on a payout, top level of a payin | When the quoted terms stop applying. |

[`POST /dapi/v1/payouts/preview`](/api-reference/transfers/create-an-outgoing-transfer-quote) prices a rail without a contact. A price from capabilities or preview is indicative. `POST /dapi/v1/payouts` repeats the checks and returns the binding quote.

To choose which side of a payment absorbs the fee, send `amount` or `amountTo` ([Conventions](/developers/conventions#money)).

### Product rate

[`GET /dapi/v1/banks/{product}/rate`](/api-reference/virtual-accounts/retrieve-the-receiving-conversion-rate) returns the indicative price of a payin on one product.

```json
{ "currency": "EUR", "rate": "…", "fixedFee": "…", "fixedFeeCurrency": "EUR" }
```

### Market rate

[`GET /dapi/v1/rates?from=EUR&to=USDC`](/api-reference/catalog/retrieve-a-market-reference-rate) returns the current market rate between two currencies or tokens.

```json
{ "fromCurrency": "EUR", "toCurrency": "USDC", "rate": "1.14776038", "indicative": true }
```

A currency HEVN does not price returns `404 not_found`.

### Not a fee

| Item | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Gas | Gas on Base is paid by an ERC-4337 paymaster. A quote has no network fee line, and the client's wallet needs no ETH. A transfer an external account sends is paid for by the customer's wallet. |
| A move with a sub account | One for one, with no fee ([Swaps](/developers/swaps#sub-account-moves)). |
| Escrow `feeBps` | Your take rate on `capture` and `charge`, deducted from the captured amount before the seller is paid. The ceiling is fixed when the deal is created. See [Escrow](/developers/escrow#the-matrix). |

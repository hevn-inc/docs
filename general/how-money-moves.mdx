---
title: "How money moves"
description: "How USDC enters and leaves a HEVN wallet, and which credential authorizes each step."
---

Money in a HEVN account is USDC in a Base smart wallet. A payin ends when USDC arrives in the wallet, and a payout starts with a transfer the holder's key signs.

## Login and authorization

The holder signs in with Privy by email or Google, and the Privy key signs a challenge message offchain at no gas cost. HEVN checks the signer against the holder's Privy address and issues a short-lived JWT.

| | Platform authorization | Money authorization |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Credential | HEVN JWT | Signature from a wallet owner key, or from a [remote transfer key](/remote-transfers/overview) within its onchain allowance |
| Grants | Contacts, invoices, contracts, account details, verification (KYC and KYB), transaction reports, analytics, balances, team administration | Transfers and any onchain state change |
| Issued by | HEVN | The signer's key, per operation |

The JWT cannot move money.

## Payins

| Source | Path |
| --- | --- |
| Bank transfer | The payer sends fiat to the account details. The partner bank converts it and delivers USDC to the wallet address. |
| USDC on Base | Send to the wallet address shown in the app. |
| Other chain or token | Send to a one-time address from a cross-chain quote. See [Cross-chain](#cross-chain). |

The partner bank opens account details (an account number or IBAN) for one holder and links them to that holder's wallet address. A payin is complete when the USDC arrives on Base.

## Fiat payouts

1. Create a payout to a bank beneficiary in the app or through the API. The JWT authorizes this call, which returns a quote and moves no money.
2. The payout returns a one-time address. The app checks its [signature](/general/security-model#signed-responses) before showing it.
3. The holder's key signs the USDC transfer from the wallet to that address.
4. After the USDC arrives, the partner bank pays the beneficiary.

Each one-time address serves one payout. The transfer is public on [Basescan](https://basescan.org).

## Cross-chain

Payins from other chains and payouts to them (BTC, ETH, SOL, USDT on TRON and others) are routed swaps.

1. HEVN requests a quote and returns a one-time address on the origin chain. The fee is in the quote.
2. The app validates the address against the quote before showing it.
3. The sender pays the one-time address, and the route delivers the destination asset.

If a swap fails, for example on an expired quote or a mismatched transfer, a payin is refunded to the sender's refund address and a payout to the wallet.

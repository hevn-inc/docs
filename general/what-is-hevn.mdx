---
title: "What is HEVN"
description: "HEVN is payments software over a Base smart wallet that the account holder controls."
---

HEVN is payments software: an API and app over a Base smart wallet that only its holder's key can sign for. HEVN is not a bank. Virtual accounts, fiat payins and fiat payouts are provided by partner banks, which hold fiat while it is in transit.

A HEVN account is a profile plus a Base smart wallet that holds USDC. The app adds contacts, invoices, contracts, payouts to bank accounts, cards issued by a partner and transaction reports.

## Self-serve and integrations

| | Self-serve | HEVN Connect | External wallets |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Account creation | The holder signs up in the HEVN app with an email | Your customer creates a HEVN account in a sign-in window on your site and links it to your product | Your backend creates a client with `POST /dapi/v1/clients` and registers a wallet address the customer holds |
| Operator | None | Your backend, with a developer key. It verifies the account, requests its virtual accounts and books its payments | Your backend, with a developer key. It verifies the client, requests its virtual accounts and books its payments |
| Payment approval | The holder, with their key | The holder, with their key, in the sign-in window | The customer, with a transfer from their own wallet |
| Docs | This tab | [HEVN Connect tab](/connect/overview) | [External wallets tab](/external-wallets/overview) |

[Build on HEVN](/general/build-on-hevn) compares the two integrations.

## Account structure

| Layer | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| Identity | The holder's email address or Google account |
| Key | A [Privy](https://privy.io) embedded wallet, unlocked by the email login |
| Funds | A Base smart wallet that holds USDC. The Privy key is its first owner, and the sole owner until the holder [adds others](/general/security-model#shared-access) |
| Platform | The HEVN API. It stores contacts, invoices and account details |

The wallet is non-custodial. HEVN holds no key to it, and spending needs a signature from an owner key. See [Security model](/general/security-model).

## Deterministic addresses

Each email maps to one Privy key, and the smart wallet address is computed from that key with `CREATE2`. HEVN can compute the address for an email before its holder signs up.

A payment sent by email goes straight to that address. USDC sent to an address before the wallet is deployed stays there until first use.

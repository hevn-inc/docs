A virtual account is requested by **product** — a region code such as `eu` or `us` — and HEVN picks the rail behind it. This page is what each product carries and what each payment method needs.

Alongside the identifiers, every requisite and every contact carries a `holder`: `type` (`individual` or `business`) with `firstName` and `lastName`, or `businessName`. Some methods route on the name alone and ignore the account identifiers for matching; the identifiers are still required.

## Products

These are the products a client can request with `POST /dapi/v1/banks`, the methods its payers use, and the currency each one settles from. A payer wires that currency; the client's wallet is credited in a stablecoin.

| `product` | Methods | Currency | Region |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `us` | `ach`, `fedwire` | USD | United States |
| `swift` | `swift` | USD | International |
| `eu` | `sepa` | EUR | Euro area |
| `uk` | `ukfps` | GBP | United Kingdom |
| `uae` | `uaefts` | AED | United Arab Emirates |
| `br` | `pix` | BRL | Brazil |
| `mx` | `spei` | MXN | Mexico |
| `co` | `breb` | COP | Colombia |
| `au` | `npp` | AUD | Australia |
| `ng` | `nip` | NGN | Nigeria |

`GET /dapi/v1/banks` is authoritative and narrower: it returns only the products a given client can request, after its residency and legal form are taken into account. Read `currency` and `method` off that response rather than deriving them from this table. The `product` enum in the API reference lists more codes than this table; a code with no rail behind it for a client is simply absent from that client's list, and requesting it answers `403 rail_not_available`.

The product is the key: the same code in every environment, and the one you send, store and put in the path. `rail` on the record names the route HEVN chose behind it. It is informational and can change while the record is `preparing` — compare nothing against it and never show it to your customers.

A named account is opened in the client's own legal name, so payers see the client and nothing else is needed for the money to be attributed. A pooled account is one partner account serving many clients; its account details carry a `paymentReference`, and each deposit is attributed by it, so the payer must quote it.

## Accounts a rail settles into

`supportedAccounts` on a virtual account is which of the client's accounts its rail can deliver into, named as `USDC` or `EURC`, and `railCurrency` is what the rail delivers.

The token is fixed by the rail, and HEVN picks the rail for the product, so it is not something you choose when you request. `destinationAccount` on the record echoes what the active account delivers.

Per deposit the choice is on the money-in call: `POST /dapi/v1/payins` takes a `destinationAccount`, and a token no rail of the client's settles into answers `422 payin_not_available` with `details.options[]` listing the pairs that do.

`feeBps` on the record is HEVN's deposit fee in basis points for that product, from the client's plan. It is a whole number: `25` is 0.25%.

## Chain codes

On-chain destinations use a chain code, not a rail: a wallet [contact](/whitelabel/payouts#save-an-address-as-a-contact) carries one in `crypto.chain`, and a quoted on-chain [payin](/whitelabel/payins#quote-an-on-chain-transfer) carries one in `originChainId`.

`GET /dapi/v1/chains` answers the live list rather than this page: every chain, its EVM `chainId`, and each token's `decimals` and contract `address`. `settlement: true` marks the chain balances live on, and a token's `account` names the account it holds when a client can hold a balance in it. Read it once at startup instead of hardcoding what follows.

`eth` · `bsc` · `avax` · `op` · `arb` · `pol` · `sol` · `base` · `gnosis` · `tron` · `xrp` · `zec` · `doge` · `ltc` · `btc` · `cardano` · `bera` · `sui` · `near` · `near_intents` · `xlayer` · `plasma` · `ton` · `stellar` · `monad` · `starknet` · `aptos` · `adi` · `bch`

A client balance settles on **Base**, in USDC or EURC, so a contact on Base holding either token is paid straight out of it. A contact on any other chain in this list, or expecting any other token, is paid through a [cross-chain route](/whitelabel/payouts#a-routed-payout-is-priced) — the same `POST /dapi/v1/payouts`, quoted with `fromChainId` and `toChainId`, refused with `409 payout_not_fundable` when routing is unavailable for the account.

## Market rates

`GET /dapi/v1/rates?from=EUR&to=USDC` converts one currency or token into another at the current market rate, before any transfer exists:

```json
{ "fromCurrency": "EUR", "toCurrency": "USDC", "rate": "1.14776038", "indicative": true }
```

Both sides accept a fiat code or a token symbol, so a fiat pair, a token pair and a mixed pair answer the same way. It is a display rate and says so: the rate a transfer settles at comes from that transfer's own quote, and a rail's deposit rate comes from `GET /dapi/v1/banks/{rail}/rate`. A currency HEVN does not price answers `404 not_found`.
